Risk-adjusted score = estimated EV × (Evidence Score ÷ 100)². This score changes only selection order, not model win probabilities or EV. Results require substantial prospective verified samples; neither list is automatically promoted.

Important model limitations. Rankings prioritize the difference between historical projected stats and sportsbook lines, adjusted for stat volatility and evidence. The gap is not a calibrated probability or proof of a profitable bet. Grades B/C/D/F reflect an experimental evidence score, not proven likelihood of winning. Grade A is withheld until sufficient out-of-sample calibration is available. Historical logs progressively grow toward 40 games per player; prior-season data is downweighted by an age penalty. Injury status, starting lineups, defensive matchups and changing odds are not independently verified. Results are not guaranteed; no shortlist means there were no qualifying props or current feed data. The tracker settles only when a corresponding player game log is found; missed games can remain pending. The refresh button reloads the latest published snapshot, not the sportsbook feed.
